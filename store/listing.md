## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
TOP SHELF is fast, old-school arcade hockey that looks and plays like a 16-bit cartridge, from the attract-mode demo behind PRESS START to the sound of the goal horn.

BEST WITH A CONTROLLER
TOP SHELF is built for game controllers. Pair a Bluetooth controller with your phone and press any button: the touch buttons step aside and the rink fills the screen. No controller? The touch controls and touch assist have you covered.

PLAY IT YOUR WAY
• Season mode: take over one of 32 clubs and chase the Silver Cup through a full season and the playoffs
• Exhibition: one game against the CPU, or two players on one device
• Online: challenge a friend with a 4-letter room code
• Period lengths from 2 to 20 minutes, three difficulty levels, and penalties and icing that can be turned on or off

ARCADE HOCKEY THE WAY YOU REMEMBER IT
• A vertical-scrolling rink that follows the puck
• One-timers, slap shots, dekes, poke checks and big open-ice hits
• Smart player switching that puts you on the defender who can stop the rush
• Three-on-three overtime and shootouts in the regular season, sudden-death overtime in the playoffs

BE THE GM
• Set your forward lines, defence pairs and goalies
• Manage contracts under a salary cap
• Make trades, sign free agents, re-sign your stars and run the draft
• Injuries, player development and retirements carry your club from season to season

THE BROADCAST BOOTH
Play-by-play man Mick Embers and analyst Eddie O'Shea call the action, with a pre-game show, intermission scores from around the league and a post-game wrap-up with the three stars.

MADE FOR PHONES AND FOLDABLES
The controls sit beside the rink in landscape and under it in portrait, and move to the other half of the screen on a half-folded phone. Every key and controller button can be rebound.

NO ADS. NO IN-APP PURCHASES. NO ACCOUNT.
Your seasons are saved on your device. All clubs, players and commentators are fictional. TOP SHELF is not affiliated with any professional league.
```
