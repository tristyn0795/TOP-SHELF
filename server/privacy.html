TOP SHELF: Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy covers the TOP SHELF website and the TOP SHELF app on Google Play.

The short version

TOP SHELF has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your season, team management, settings and control bindings are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes them.

Online play

When you play ONLINE, your game inputs (stick and button presses) and a random room code pass through our game server so the two players' games stay in sync. Nothing identifies you. The room and everything in it are deleted when the game ends, either player leaves, or the room sits idle for 30 minutes.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider (Railway) may briefly keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, time and the page requested, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use them to identify or profile anyone.

Microphone

The Google Play app never asks for the microphone. The website has an optional recording studio for commentary lines. If you use it, recordings are made and stored only in your browser on your device, and are only shared if you choose to download and upload them yourself.

Vibration and controllers

The game can vibrate your phone on goals and big hits (you can turn this off in OPTIONS) and reads game controllers you connect. Neither involves personal data.

Children

TOP SHELF is a sports game suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
